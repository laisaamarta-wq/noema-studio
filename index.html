(02) Manifesto

We don't build websites. We give thoughts a form you can feel, on the thin line where design, code, machine intelligence and art melt into one material.

Noema, n.Greek: that which is thought. The object of a thought, the moment before it becomes a thing.

MethodThought, then form, then feeling. Every project moves through the same three states.
